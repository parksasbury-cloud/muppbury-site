Delete your Perfect Tan account and data

Perfect Tan is published by MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC. You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Perfect Tan and go to the Profile screen.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and all associated cloud data (profile, tanning sessions and group membership) right away.

By email

If you can't use the app, email eatsaiofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Perfect Tan account" from the email address on your account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account (name, email, user ID), tanning sessions synced to the cloud, skin profile, and group memberships.
  • Not stored by us: selfie photos and your precise location are never stored on our servers.
  • Kept: purchase records held by Google Play or Apple, which those stores keep under their own policies. Cancel subscriptions in your store account; deleting your Perfect Tan account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

See our privacy policy for details.
