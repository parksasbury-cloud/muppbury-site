Common questions

How do I replay the tutorial? Open Settings, then How to play. Ad privacy choices are in Settings too.

I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the same Apple ID.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
