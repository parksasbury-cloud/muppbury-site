Critter Grid Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. Critter Grid is published by MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC ("we").

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or account details, and Critter Grid has no sign-in. Your puzzle progress, album, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for hints. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data about ad performance. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the app's settings. Critter Grid does not use Apple's advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Remove Ads purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only stores on your device that ads have been removed.

Children

Critter Grid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset ad personalization from your device settings and the in-app privacy option, buy Remove Ads to turn off full-screen ads, or delete the app to remove all data it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
