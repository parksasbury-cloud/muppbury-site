Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. Applies to browser extensions published by MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. Our extensions work entirely inside your browser. Files and pages you open, and your settings, stay on your device (settings may sync through your own browser account via Chrome's storage). We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and we do not sell or share data.

Payments

If you buy a Pro upgrade, the payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They receive the details needed to process the payment and tell the extension that Pro is unlocked; we never see your card details.

Permissions

Each extension asks only for the permissions it needs to work, as listed on its Chrome Web Store page.

Our Pinterest scheduling app

We use a private Pinterest app (Muppbury Pin Scheduler) only with our own Pinterest business account, to create and schedule Pins for our own shop's products and to read our own Pins, boards and analytics. It does not access other people's Pinterest data, and we do not sell or share any data from it. Access tokens are stored privately and you can ask us any question at the contact below.
