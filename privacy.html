Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Applies to browser extensions published by MUPPBURY HOLDINGS LLC ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. Our extensions work entirely inside your browser. Files and pages you open, and your settings, stay on your device (settings may sync through your own browser account via Chrome's storage). We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and we do not sell or share data.

Payments

If you buy a Pro upgrade, the payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They receive the details needed to process the payment and tell the extension that Pro is unlocked; we never see your card details.

Permissions

Each extension asks only for the permissions it needs to work, as listed on its Chrome Web Store page.

Our Pinterest scheduling app

We use a private Pinterest app (Muppbury Pin Scheduler) only with our own Pinterest business account, to create and schedule Pins for our own shop's products and to read our own Pins, boards and analytics. It does not access other people's Pinterest data, and we do not sell or share any data from it. Access tokens are stored privately and you can ask us any question at the contact below.

Tab Sessions

Tab Sessions saves the tab information you choose to keep, plus automatic snapshots when enabled. It stores session names, tab URLs and titles, favicon references, tab order, pinned and active states, window and group structure, group colors, folders, tags, timestamps and settings in your Chrome profile. A close-recovery feature keeps a working copy of your open windows on your device. Incognito access is disabled.

The extension has no analytics, advertising, developer server or remote code, and it does not read the contents of web pages. Restoring a tab opens the saved address, so that site receives an ordinary browser visit. Export creates a JSON, Markdown or HTML file where you choose to save it; these files may contain private URLs and page titles. Import reads only the file you select.

"Back up to Chrome sync" copies your saved sessions into Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may transfer this copy through your Google account according to your browser settings. It runs only when you request it, and we never receive it.

You can turn scheduled saves and close recovery off independently in Settings, set how many automatic snapshots are kept, and delete sessions at any time. Manual sessions stay until you delete them or remove the extension. Permissions: tabs (read tab titles and addresses, open restored tabs), tabGroups (read and recreate tab groups), storage (save sessions and settings, optional sync backup), alarms (schedule automatic snapshots). It requests no host permissions.
